This project is a web app for assisting and facilitating NFL fantasy football drafts. The core functionality is an online ranking list where users can choose from several different "Top 300" lists,
such as ESPN's PPR top 300, and the users can then simply drag and drop players around according to how they see fit. For example, if they believe that the number 4 overall pick should really be the 2nd overall pick,
they can just simply click and drag that player up into the 2nd spot. This allows for unlimited customizable freedom with draft ranking lists instead of using a universal list as most fantasy football 
fans do every season. To make this app more functional, there is a shared draft lobby where friends can all join together via an invite link and make their picks in a specified draft order. The app shows realtime updates just like google docs shows when a collaborator makes a change.

1. Responsive Web App that can be viewed in browser as well as cleanly on mobile devices.
2. Users can make an account and save their changes/fantasy draft prep materials. Their account will also allow them to join draft links with friends (see item #3 below).
3. Nice, large, and most importantly, shared visual draft board that users can see after joining a "draft lobby" with their friends via invite links.
4. Options for league size from 6 to 14 teams, roster/bench sizes, and custom rosters (i.e. 2QB, or 3 WR, etc.).
5. Ability to custom rank players in a list by clicking and dragging, with a toggleable lock button functionality that prevents players from being moved in a ranking list by default.
6. Timed draft capabilities within the app so a user can join a shared lobby with other users, start a draft, and after a certain time limit expires, the user who was supposed to pick their next player
    will have their pick automatically selected from their own custom list that they modified and curated within the app. (This is a pain point in most draft softwares because they will just force
    the top available player according to say ESPN's rankings to a user's team if the time limit expires. We want more choice in that draft selection via custom ranking lists.)


Future Items to Consider:
1. A chatbot that can pull from historical data and let you know what a player's stat trends are over time.
2. An advanced statistics page that shows graphs of trends in a player's stats from season to season or even game to game.
3. An AI analysis of a user's custom ranking list. It could give a grade based off of which draft pick position this user will be drafting from. 
4. A one-click "health check" that serves as a sanity check for users to see if this player they are about to draft has been injured in the last 12 months and checks recent tweets for injury news about them.
